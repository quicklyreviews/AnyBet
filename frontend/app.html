How to play

  1. Connect a wallet

    Any EVM wallet. StudioNet is added for you, and Get test GEN tops you up. Nothing here costs real money.

  2. Deposit once

    Move GEN into the contract. Bets are staked from that balance, so placing one transfers nothing.

  3. Back YES or NO

    The pool minus the fee splits across the winning side by stake, so the price moves as money arrives.

  4. Anyone resolves

    After close, one click sends every validator to fetch the sources and judge. They must agree, or it voids.

  5. Collect

    Winnings are recorded, not pushed. You claim them, which is what makes a win something you noticed.

Every step waits about a minute for consensus — that is GenLayer agreeing, not the page hanging. Betting closes 75 seconds early for the same reason.
