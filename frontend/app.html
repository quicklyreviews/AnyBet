Ask the question, say how it should be judged, and name where to look. At close, every validator fetches those sources and runs the same prompt — and unless they agree, nobody is paid and everybody is refunded. How the consensus works, and why strict_eq would have been the wrong choice.

How to take part

  1. Connect an EVM wallet

    StudioNet is added for you. The page holds no keys — your wallet signs each action.

  2. Take test GEN, then deposit

    Bets are staked from a balance inside the contract, so deposit once and each bet costs no transfer.

  3. Back a side, or open a market

    The pool minus the fee splits across the winning side by stake, so the price moves as money arrives.

Every step waits about a minute for consensus — that is GenLayer agreeing, not the page hanging. Betting closes 75 seconds early for the same reason.
