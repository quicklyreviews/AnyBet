How cover works here

  1. Name the trigger

    The event you want cover against, how it should be judged, and where to look. No product catalogue to pick from.

  2. Pay a premium

    The pool reserves your full payout the moment the policy is written, so it can never sell cover it could not honour.

  3. Nobody files anything

    At expiry anyone triggers settlement. Validators fetch your sources and adjudicate. There is no assessor to convince.

  4. Three ways it ends

    Fired pays the sum insured. Not fired earns the pool your premium. Unknowable refunds you in full.

That last one is the part that is not like insurance. Ordinarily doubt favours the insurer and an unprovable claim is denied. Here, if the evidence cannot settle it, the pool earns nothing and your premium comes back — possible only because the adjudicator works for neither side.
